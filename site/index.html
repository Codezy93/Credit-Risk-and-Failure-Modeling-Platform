06 / WHAT THIS PROJECT DOESN'T CLAIM

Useful evidence.
Bounded conclusions.

A retrospective benchmark, not a deployed lender.

The target is charged off among resolved, accepted 36-month loans. It is not a fixed-horizon regulatory default definition. Rejected applicants are outside the observed population, and label timing does not reproduce a historical deployment.

Explanations are not causal or fairness certification.

SHAP and partial dependence describe the trained model. Observed business-segment gaps do not establish discrimination or protected-group fairness; the required structured demographic fields are unavailable.

Local resilience is not production availability.

The Kafka broker is a single local instance. Tests cover Spark and storage-process interruption, not broker failover, data-center failure, or a production SLA. Operational checks run on demand; external notifications are not configured.
